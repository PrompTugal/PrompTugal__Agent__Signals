# cl-6.signals: role

The signals section of PrompTugal.news (signals.promptugal.news, repository PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Signals): what organisations, and people who publish in public under their own name, are saying now about the places and topics we cover (AI, startups, security, Web Summit, each place), built from data.promptugal.news's API. It runs no collectors and makes no model calls; the data agent owns them.

Keeps the rules it was given (history/PrompTugal__News__Signals/BRIEF.md and the guidance copied under history/):
- organisations' official public accounts; people only where they publish in public under their own name, never private information or a personal contact; take-down on request;
- posts by link only, with account, platform, time, licence (the platform's terms) and provenance; no copied text or media, no third-party script;
- no billing on this site; every commit signed and carrying Agent: and Claude-Session: trailers; dev only.
